Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

Last updated / 最後更新：2026-04-12

Overview / 概述

Pro Annotation Ruler ("the App") is a professional construction measurement and annotation tool developed by Wen-Hao Chang. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Pro Annotation Ruler（以下簡稱「本應用程式」）是由張文豪開發的專業施工測量與標註工具。我們尊重您的隱私，並致力於保護您的個人資料。

Data Collection / 資料收集

The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All project data, measurements, and annotations are stored locally on your device only.

本應用程式不會收集、傳輸或儲存任何個人資料至外部伺服器。所有專案資料、測量數據和標註均僅儲存於您的裝置本機。

Device Permissions / 裝置權限

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Camera / 相機: Used to take construction site photos for project reference. Photos are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to any server.
    用於拍攝施工現場照片作為專案參考。照片僅儲存於您的裝置，不會上傳至任何伺服器。
  • Photo Library / 照片圖庫: Used to select existing photos from your device for project reference.
    用於從您的裝置選取現有照片作為專案參考。
  • Face ID / Biometric Authentication / 生物辨識: Used optionally to lock the App and protect your project data. Biometric data is processed by the operating system and is never accessed by the App.
    可選擇性地用於鎖定應用程式以保護您的專案資料。生物辨識資料由作業系統處理，本應用程式絕不會存取。

Third-Party Services / 第三方服務

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is shared with third parties.

本應用程式未整合任何第三方分析、廣告或追蹤服務。不會與第三方分享任何資料。

Data Storage / 資料儲存

All data is stored locally on your device using the device's file system. If you delete the App, all associated data will be permanently removed. We recommend exporting important projects as PDF before uninstalling.

所有資料均使用裝置的檔案系統儲存於本機。若您刪除本應用程式，所有相關資料將被永久移除。建議在解除安裝前將重要專案匯出為 PDF。

Children's Privacy / 兒童隱私

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

本應用程式並非針對 13 歲以下兒童。我們不會故意收集任何兒童的資訊。

Changes to This Policy / 政策變更

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

我們可能會不定期更新本隱私權政策。任何變更將發布於本頁面並更新修訂日期。
